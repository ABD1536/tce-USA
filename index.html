Everything you need to know about our creator network, performance attribution, and campaign execution.

We create high-impact campaigns by partnering with popular online creators whose followers already match your ideal customers, letting them showcase your product naturally instead of reading a boring script. First, we set clear goals like boosting sales or spreading brand awareness and handle all the behind-the-scenes work. Once the best posts are out, we put an extra advertising budget behind the top performers so even more people see them, then measure the exact results so you know your money was well spent.

Every creator is assessed on real engagement, audience quality, and how well their followers match the people you're trying to reach. We also check past campaign performance and screen out fake followers and inflated numbers, so you're only matched with creators whose influence is genuine and whose audience actually fits your brand.

Our fastest turnaround from brief to launch is 72 hours, including creator matching, contracts, and content approvals. You can expect your first creator shortlist within 24 hours.

We track your campaign's live performance through a central dashboard that instantly shows where your money goes and what it earns back. By assigning each creator unique promo codes and direct links, we trace sales straight to the specific post that generated them. At the same time, the system monitors exact engagement costs, showing what you pay per like, comment or share and measures the total exposure value compared to traditional advertising. This gives you full, end to end visibility into the viewer's path from initial discovery to final purchase, ensuring complete transparency and proven returns every step of the way.
